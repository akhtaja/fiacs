# Product Overview

## Name
FIA Consultancy Services (fiacs)

## Description
Static website for FIA Consultancy Services — an IT consultancy based in Belgium — plus a kids gamification app ("Adventure Quest") for tracking good deeds.

## Domain
www.fiacs.be

## Key Pages
- **index.html** — Main business page (services, about, contact)
- **kids.html** — Adventure Quest gamification app for Hamzah & Yahya (points, levels, milestones)

## Target Users
- Potential business clients looking for IT consultancy services
- Family (kids) using the Adventure Quest tracker

## Current Features
- IT services showcase (infrastructure, cloud, cybersecurity, digital strategy)
- Contact info with email and phone
- Scroll-reveal animations
- Kids point tracker with localStorage persistence, levels, milestone track, team quests
